## Risks and explicit output policy

- [gifuct-js issue #35](https://github.com/matt-way/gifuct-js/issues/35): GIF disposal belongs to the **preceding** painted frame; disposal=2 applies to its **rectangle**, not the whole logical screen. gifuct decompression returns patch indices/offsets, **not** a composited frame.
- [gifenc issue #15](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc/issues/15): transparent input and disposal=2 may stack unexpectedly. We compose source patches explicitly with restore-to-previous snapshots (disposal=3), then emit *fully opaque* full-canvas GIF frames. Transparent input is matted onto **white**, explicitly shown in UI and returned metadata. It does **not** claim alpha preservation. Palette is exact when ≤128 distinct RGB colors, otherwise limited to 128 colors with **declared possible color loss**.
- Source timing comes from our structure inspector's raw **centiseconds**, not gifuct-js fallback for zero delay; delays below 2 cs (20ms) are refused to avoid silent browser clamping. Netscape loop extension is preserved (null means no loop extension). Max source 4MiB, output 6MiB, 30 frames, 256×256 canvas, 10s total, 1,966,080 decoded pixel-frames. Abort checked before read and during pixel reconstruction.
- First-frame export remains a **separate** explicitly lossy operation. Static GIF and unsupported geometry/timing are rejected on the animated endpoint. No silent single-GIF fallback, no uploads and no production deploy.

Independent GIF fixtures are generated by scripts/acceptance-gif-animation-273.py with Pillow, covering disposal 0/1/2/3, nonzero partial offsets, loop 2 and no loop, interlaced 8×8 rows, raw delays, alpha flattened over white. The **actual reencoded GIF** is decoded independently by Pillow, with per-frame RGB pixel comparison after the declared white matte, timing, logical geometry and loop equality. tests/gif-animation-273.test.ts covers truncation, malformed source, over-byte/pixel, sub-20ms delay, static input, abort and preservation of #212. scripts/acceptance-gif-animation-273.browser.ts validates the public Chromium UI, downloaded file and external Pillow consumer.
