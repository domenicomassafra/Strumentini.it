## Engine, Gold and dependency decisions (23 September 2026)

- The framework-neutral core spike implemented ten zero-dependency engine families and reported 38 passing checks then. Its maintained implementation, current reproducibility commands and ceilings are in `spikes/core-engines/README.md` and `spikes/core-engines/DESIGN.md`. Date arithmetic excludes holidays without versioned data, `SubtleCrypto.digest` is not streaming, and text-diff LCS has a bounded matrix. This experimental result alone never promotes a production engine.
- The original Gold factory used 44 fixture families and independent source/expected-output evidence for 200 candidates. The **living** authorities are `quality/oracles/{README.md,registry.json,sources.json}`, `quality/fixtures/` and their validators, not the retired bootstrap. Normative vectors outrank hand-worked literals, independent implementation cross-checks and invariants; the implementation being tested cannot manufacture its own expected Gold result. External ISO conformity corpora were not copied without rights.
- **RIS policy (`ris-policy`):** RIS has uneven publicly available vendor lineage and no single freely accessible complete normative grammar in this research. Use synthetic RIS records plus an independent parser cross-check; preserve unknown tags. This is explicitly a *project policy*, not a claim of normative certification. This paragraph is the human-readable locator for `quality/oracles/sources.json`.
- The browser dependency spike favored native Canvas/ImageBitmap for bounded JPEG/WebP resize, an incremental SHA-256 worker for large blobs, and separately evaluated `hash-wasm` against `@noble/hashes`. Complex AVIF/PDF cases required later feasibility checks. Refer to current `src/engines/`, `spikes/`, project licences, oracles and relevant GitHub tickets rather than September version/size figures.

## Heavy local processing (6 October 2026)

- The reproducible test corpus and exact historical measurements are in `spikes/heavy-local-feasibility/` and `quality/benchmarks/heavy-local-feasibility/`. Its five **desktop** engines were Chrome, Edge, Playwright Chromium, Firefox and WebKit. Desktop WebKit does not prove iOS Safari. Chromium JS heap excludes WASM/native/GPU/process memory. Lack of physical Android/iOS receipts prohibits inferring mobile acceptance.
- Tickets **267–275** resolved feasibility only and **refused production promotion** at that checkpoint: P7M/CMS could parse SignedData but not establish chain trust; codecs/PDF/Office needed stronger fidelity, safety or mobile evidence; OCR was local in a desktop corpus but not approved for mobile; ZIP/GZIP required traversal/bomb protection and cancellable workers; audio/video codec and transcoding claims were unsupported; EML/MSG/EPUB had no approved semantic parser. The recorded decision receipt is `quality/benchmarks/heavy-local-feasibility/ticket-decisions-267-275.json`.
- Licences and safety barriers were substantive: `@ffmpeg/core@0.12.10` was GPL-2.0-or-later and denied under the repository policy; `xlsx@0.18.5` and `pptxgenjs@4.0.1` were denied on the inspected advisory/dependency graphs; `libheif-js@1.23.5` had an LGPL packaging review; `pkijs@3.4.1` was BSD-3-Clause and `fflate@0.8.3` MIT in isolated spikes only. The `fflate` corpus exposed an unsafe `../escape.txt` name and high compression ratios. **Rerun upstream legal/security assessment on exact versions before any future adoption.**

## Refused language and astronomy features (6 October 2026)

- **#309 — Italian syllabification: REFUSE authoritative division.** TeX pattern hyphenation is not complete phonological/orthographic syllabification. In the dated `hyphen@1.14.1` Italian test, `amido`, `mia`, `leone`, `aiuola` and `nessun'amica` missed or altered boundaries relative to Treccani. The engine was ISC; Italian hyphenation patterns documented dual LPPL-1.3-or-later/MIT terms, which require independent redistribution review. Keep the held-out `quality/fixtures/italian-syllabification/corpus.json`. Primary sources: [Treccani grammar](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/divisione-in-sillabe_%28La-grammatica-italiana%29/), [Treccani prontuario](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/divisione-in-prontuario-sillabe_%28Enciclopedia-dell%27Italiano%29/), [CTAN patterns](https://ctan.org/pkg/hyph-utf8). Reopen only for a maintained syllabification-specific oracle or a clearly labeled typographic line-breaking feature.
- **#311 — seasons, Sun and Moon: REFUSE production routes in this tranche.** Accurate offline results require a versioned ephemeris/algorithm, defined years, independent vectors, time scales, atmospheric/refraction choices and polar edge-case policy. [USNO](https://aa.usno.navy.mil/data/api.html) is an authoritative remote data service; [NOAA's solar calculation notes](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html) describe a legacy unmaintained calculator with conditional theoretical accuracy. Coordinates combined with dates can reveal routines: keep them local by default; never leak them to URLs, analytics or an external service without a distinct informed opt-in. A separately approved remote mode would need its own privacy and capability review.
