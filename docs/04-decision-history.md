# Decision history

## Origin

UtilityOS originated inside the research-only ProgettoAmbizioso master while exploring software assets that could be built once, remain useful for years and operate with extremely low recurring compute/token cost.

## Rejected as core identities

### Generic universal toolbox
Rejected because the market already contains many large browser/client-side suites. Useful architecture, weak thesis.

### Italy-first calculator/fiscal toolbox
Rejected because Italy already has broad calculator/admin suites. Such tools may still exist as optional modules/packs.

### Transparent/versioned calculator platform
Rejected as sufficient differentiation. Visible formulas, citations and historical rules are quality features, not unique positioning.

### Universal-moment toolbox
A map of 100 recurring digital moments was useful for discovery, but too abstract as product identity.

### MoveProof / DeleteSafe
Migration/deletion proof was the strongest moment-specific candidate for a time, but too specific to define the whole product. It remains a possible module.

## Current thesis

Adaptive role-aware utility workspace for knowledge workers, with independent knowledge workers as the first research wedge.

Decision status: PROMOTED TO SEPARATE REPOSITORY FOR PRODUCT RESEARCH.

This promotion does not mean product-market fit or direct novelty has been proven.
