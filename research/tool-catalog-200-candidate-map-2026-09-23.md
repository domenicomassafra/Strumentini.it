## Catalog observations

This first 200 intentionally favors deterministic and evergreen jobs.

It does NOT yet include:

- heavy video/audio conversion;
- broad OCR;
- AI writing/generation;
- country-specific tax depth;
- legal advice;
- health calculators with meaningful risk;
- social-media downloading;
- scraping tools that create operational/legal fragility.

Those can be researched later as separate capability classes.

## Why 200 can still be coherent

The catalog is not the product surface.

The person sees:

- a starter set;
- installed packs;
- recent tools;
- search.

The 200-tool library is the supply behind personalization.

## Next validation

For every candidate:

1. search direct incumbents;
2. estimate real task frequency;
3. assign maintenance class;
4. identify implementation primitive;
5. identify independent correctness oracle;
6. decide Gold-quality acceptance criteria;
7. assign to zero, one or more role packs.

Reject duplicates and low-value keyword variants aggressively.
