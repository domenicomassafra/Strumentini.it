It validates `stsz` sample count, `stsc` chunk assignment, `stco/co64` offsets in file, `stsd` description index, `mdhd` timescale, `mvhd` timebase and single **identity** edit-list timing (media time 0, playback rate 1×, duration matching decoded samples with declared timescale rounding); all non-identity/duplicate/malformed edits, fragments, ambiguous or signed-negative composition offsets, >240fps/<1fps sparse rates and over-budget metadata remain **unknown**. Per-track expansion max 100,000 frames; aggregate expansion max 200,000 across tracks; source-only metadata reads `moov` max 8 MiB. CFR FPS comes from **verified constant PTS step**, not average fps or a claim of frame decoding; VFR presents variable PTS without inventing average FPS.
