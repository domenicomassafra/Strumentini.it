## Limits / gate

Supported format is the **strictly parsed classic RIFF WAVE subset**: 8–48 kHz, mono/stereo, basic 16-byte fmt chunk, at most one data chunk and safe ancillary chunks, 12 MiB/source, 24 MiB combined, 60s/output and 12 MiB rendered cap. Unsupported codec formats, encrypted/hosted processing, arbitrary MP3/AAC, source-rate mismatches, RF64 and advanced WAV extensions are refused. The verified output format always remains WAV signed PCM16. Truncation from 24/32 bit and floats quantizes samples. The existing #230 refusal is unchanged.
